Internet | 'intǝnƐt |

noun (the Internet)

a global computer network providing a variety of information and communication facilities, consisting of interconnected networks using standardized communication protocols: the guide is also available on the Internet | [as modifier]: Internet access | an Internet address.

origin

1970s (denoting a computer network connecting two or more smaller networks): from inter- 'reciprocal, mutual' + network.
